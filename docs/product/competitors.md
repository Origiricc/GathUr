# Competitive Landscape

_Adjacent products researched during V1 planning, and how GathUr differs._

| Product            | What it is                                                                                                          | Gap vs GathUr                                                                        |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Ekklesia**       | "The Church Community App Your Congregation Will Actually Use" — church discovery, events, DMs, prayer, social feed | Christian social app; no matching engine, no "find your people," no friendship graph |
| **UniChurch**      | Interests, member profiles, discover people, church groups — conceptually closest                                   | No relationship engine or discipleship intelligence                                  |
| **churchme**       | Facebook Group + church directory + prayer board (announcements, directory, photos, events, devotionals)            | Feels administrative, not relational                                                 |
| **FaithHaven**     | Helps you find the right _church_ via compatibility scoring (theology, worship style, values)                       | Church finder, not community builder                                                 |
| **Covenant Match** | Church + dating + pastor verification (pastors approve members)                                                     | Different market                                                                     |

## GathUr's differentiation

1. **Relationship/matching engine** — transparent recommendations for people, groups, and gatherings based on interests, life stage, ministries, service times, campus, and goals.
2. **Community health intelligence for leaders** — derived engagement states (new / unconnected / drifting / looking), connection scores, recommended actions, and per-person attendee journeys. No competitor measures _belonging_.
3. **The follow-up loop** — gatherings feed "people you met," follow-ups are assignable and tracked; connection is treated as a process, not a feed.
