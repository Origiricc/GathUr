## Onboarding Journey (7 steps)

1. **Join Your Church** — search and select your church (name, address, photo card), then create a simple profile: first/last name, email, optional date of birth. Visitor entry point is a QR code at church.
2. **Tell GathUr What You're Looking For** — multi-select: Meet People, Find a Group, Attend Gatherings, Get More Involved. Editable anytime.
3. **Get Personalized Recommendations** — "Recommended for You": a person to meet, a group to join, an upcoming gathering, each with a one-tap action (Connect / Join / RSVP) and an "explore all" escape hatch.
4. **Connect** — accept introductions ("Sarah invited you to connect with James"), view rich person cards (church, location, bio, interest tags), Connect / Message / Join a Group / Not now.
5. **Gather** — browse upcoming gatherings (official church events and casual ones: coffee, pickleball, Bible study, dinner, workouts) with photo cards and RSVP.
6. **Follow Up** — after a gathering: "You met 3 new people" with per-person follow-up actions (Connect / Message / Invite).
7. **Belong** — personal community dashboard: connections count, groups joined, upcoming gatherings, encouragement messaging ("Your presence matters").

## Key Member Surfaces

- **Home dashboard** — "Welcome back, [name]. Here are your next best steps this week": people to meet, group to join, gathering to attend, follow-up prompts. Bottom nav: Home · Connections · Groups (or Discover) · Events · Profile.
- **Person recommendation** — full-card view: shared church/campus, "N things in common" with interest chips (Young Adults, Fitness, Coffee, Worship), mutual connections, "Why you may connect" checklist (same life stage, shared interests, attends the same gatherings). Actions: Connect / Invite to Coffee / Not now.
- **Connection map** — visual graph of how the member connects through people, groups, and gatherings; stats (connections, groups, mutual connections, suggested intros) and "people you may want to meet."
- **Create a group** — any member can start one: name, who it's for (age group), category, description, meeting time, location, Public/Private visibility, suggested invitees. Create or Save as Draft.
- **Discover / search** — search groups and events with age-group filter chips: Middle School, High School, Young Adults (18–29), Adults (30–59), Seniors. Cards carry age-group badges.

## Design implications

- Age-group targeting is a first-class attribute on groups and events (badges + filters).
- Recommendations always explain themselves ("why you may connect") — transparent logic, no black box.
- Every surface ends in one obvious action (Connect, Join, RSVP, Follow up).
- Follow-up after gatherings is a core loop, not an afterthought — attendance feeds "people you met."
